## Using Video Upload

### For Admins:

1. Open `index.html` → Login as admin
2. Go to **Videos** tab
3. Fill in video details:
   - Title
   - Creator name
   - Category
4. **Choose upload method**: Select "رفع ملف فيديو" (Upload video file)
5. **Click "اختيار ملف"** - Select your video (max 200MB)
6. **Click "📤 رفع الفيديو إلى Imgur"** button
7. **Wait for upload** - Progress bar will show upload status
8. You'll see ✅ "تم رفع الفيديو بنجاح!" when complete
9. Fill in remaining fields (thumbnail, description)
10. Click **"نشر الفيديو"**

### For Users:

- Videos uploaded by admin will play automatically
- Works on all devices and browsers
- No special configuration needed

## Video Requirements

- **Maximum size:** 200MB per video
- **Supported formats:** MP4, MOV, AVI, WMV, FLV, WebM
- **Recommended format:** MP4 (H.264) for best compatibility
- **Recommended resolution:** 720p or 1080p

## Tips for Smaller Videos

If your video is larger than 200MB, you can compress it:

### Windows:

- Use **Handbrake** (free): https://handbrake.fr/
- Settings: MP4, H.264, RF 23, 720p

### Online Tools:

- https://www.freeconvert.com/video-compressor
- https://www.videosmaller.com/

## Troubleshooting

### "حجم الفيديو كبير جداً!"

- Your video is over 200MB
- Compress it using tools above
- Or split into parts

### Upload stuck at 0%?

- Check internet connection
- Try a different browser
- Make sure video file is not corrupted

### "فشل في رفع الفيديو"

- Video might be too large
- Check if file format is supported
- Try different video file

### Video won't play on user page?

- Check if upload completed successfully
- Try refreshing the page
- Make sure video URL was saved (check admin panel)

## Privacy & Security

- Videos are uploaded to Imgur's public CDN
- Anyone with the URL can view the video
- Not recommended for private/sensitive content
- Perfect for educational/public content

## Limitations

- 200MB per video (can be overcome with compression)
- Videos are publicly accessible (by URL)
- No video analytics
- No adaptive streaming (but works fine for most cases)

## Need More Space?

If you need larger videos:

1. Compress videos before uploading (recommended)
2. Split long videos into parts
3. Use YouTube for very large content (paste YouTube links instead)
